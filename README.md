# 🚚 FleetHub 2.0

## Multi-Client Delivery & Logistics Management Platform

FleetHub 2.0 is a MERN-based multi-client delivery logistics platform designed for small cafés and restaurants that cannot afford their own delivery infrastructure.

Restaurants and cafés can request delivery services through FleetHub, while FleetHub manages the delivery infrastructure including drivers, vehicles, dispatch operations, and delivery tracking.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Many small restaurants and cafés face difficulties managing:

- Delivery drivers
- Delivery vehicles
- Driver salaries
- Vehicle maintenance
- Driver availability
- Delivery assignment
- Daily delivery operations

FleetHub provides these services through a shared delivery logistics platform.

### Business Flow

```text

        ↓

        ↓
